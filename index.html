A curved label never fits in one frame. So Glyph walks around it.

A bottle label bends away from the camera at both edges. Glossy ones catch the light. The far side of a 2‑inch vial is out of focus while the near side is sharp. As you sweep the phone across the bottle, Glyph fires the shutter several times, each frame catching the slice of label that is facing the lens. It reads the text in every frame on the device and then reconciles them: where frames disagree, the reading that appears most often wins, and the field is marked as less certain.

  • Burst, not a snapshot. Several frames from slightly different angles, taken while you sweep.
  • Text is read on your phone. Only the text goes to our server to be structured. Photos stay on the device and are not stored.
  • Abbreviations expanded. SID, BID, TID, q8h, PO, PRN, EOD, "x14d" become plain words and real clock times.
  • Doubt is shown, not hidden. A field that read differently across frames, or didn't appear at all, comes up amber for you to check.
