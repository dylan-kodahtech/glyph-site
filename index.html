From label to reminder in under a minute

Vet labels are small, printed sideways, and half the words are abbreviations. Glyph does the reading so you don't have to.

Scan

Hold the phone over the label. Glyph takes several frames and reconciles them, so a crooked or glossy label still reads.

Confirm

You see what was read: drug, dose, how often, how long. Anything the scan wasn't sure about is highlighted. Nothing is saved until you say so.

Remind

Each dose gets its own time and its own reminder on your phone. Mark it given with one tap, or undo if you tapped by mistake.

One schedule for everyone who gives the meds

Invite a partner, a dog walker, or the sitter with a six-character code. They see what's due and mark it given. You see that it happened. Only you can add or change a prescription.

Who can do what

OwnerScans labels, edits schedules, invites and removes caregivers, keeps the account.
CaregiverSees today's doses for the pets shared with them and marks doses as given. Never pays.
Multiple petsEach pet has its own schedule. One scan can be given to more than one pet in the same house.

You confirm every label before it becomes a schedule. Glyph reads labels; it does not prescribe, change doses, or give medical advice. It is for medications and supplements a veterinarian prescribed or recommended for an animal in your care.
